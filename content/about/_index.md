Autumn and the Pinholes started in a village hall during a particularly wet October, when Riley had a shoebox of half-finished songs, Josh had come along for the biscuits, and nobody had a gig booked. The cat, Autumn, came along for the warmth.

We play modern folk: two people, a guitar, a melodica that Riley refuses to put down, and one cat who has opinions about the tempo. We sing about allotments, bad weather, busted bicycles, the moon, and the odd unfortunate badger.

The name comes from the cat. Autumn was here first, and the Pinholes are the rest of us. It is also what the light looks like when it comes through a tiny hole in a shed wall. We think that is a good way to make a record.

We are happiest in small rooms with wonky floorboards, and we will play in your kitchen if you ask nicely and promise not to mind the cat.
